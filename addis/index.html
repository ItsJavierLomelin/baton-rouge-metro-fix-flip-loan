Questions before you submit

Are you a direct lender?

No. We are connectors. We introduce investors to funding sources, and the funding source makes its own decisions and provides the actual terms.

What should I include about the project?

Start with the property address, purchase details, renovation plan and intended resale. If a detail is still unknown, explain what you are checking.

How fast can funding happen?

Our goal is funding within 48 hours once the project is clear and the documents are in order. Actual timing depends on the property and the funding source.

Does submitting a project guarantee funding?

No. A submission starts a review and a possible introduction. It does not guarantee a match, an approval or a particular outcome.
